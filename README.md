## How to choose a Hyperliquid app

- **Custody.** In a self-custodial app your funds stay in your own Hyperliquid account and you sign with your own wallet. Check whether an app asks you to deposit into its own account instead.
- **Agent wallets.** Many apps trade for you through an agent (API) wallet that you approve. Hyperliquid agent wallets can place orders but cannot withdraw funds. You can see and revoke them in the official app.
- **Builder fees.** Frontends can add a builder fee on top of Hyperliquid's own fees, and you approve a maximum rate once. Compare each app's fee page, not its marketing.
- **Copy rules.** For copy trading, check how size, leverage, slippage and the leader's exits are handled, and whether you can set a maximum loss.
- **Track records.** Prefer strategies and traders with long, public records. Paper results and short live histories are useful but not proof.

## Risk and safety

- Leveraged perps can be liquidated quickly. Start small and know your liquidation price.
- Copying a trader copies their drawdowns too. Past results do not predict future results.
- Revoke agent wallets and builder-fee approvals you no longer use.
- Install apps only from official sites and app-store links. Fake apps and phishing sites copy popular Hyperliquid brands.
- Nothing in this list is investment advice.
